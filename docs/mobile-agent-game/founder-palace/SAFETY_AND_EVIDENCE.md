# Safety and evidence policy

## Claim ladder

| Level | Allowed wording | Required support |
|---|---|---|
| Observation | “The accepted ledger shows…” | source, timestamp, freshness |
| Hypothesis | “One explanation may be…” | assumptions, evidence for/against, confidence |
| Recommendation | “Given objective X, consider…” | provenance, tradeoffs, alternatives, uncertainty |
| Commitment | “You approved: if X, then Y” | explicit approval receipt and exact scope |
| Outcome | “System Z recorded…” | authoritative receipt, time, source |

Never collapse these levels. Interface emphasis, coach persona, or model confidence cannot create authority.

## Evidence envelope

Every recommendation presents: objective, guardrails, source links, observation times, missing data, evidence class, confidence and basis, alternatives, expected signal, confounds, downside, approval requirement, and next review time.

Primary external validation includes willingness-to-pay, retention and margin, but evidence also records customer outcomes, cash/runway, legal and ethical constraints, strategic fit, quality, impact, confounds and confidence. Revenue is not morality, causality, durable value, or learning mastery.

## Prohibited claims

No claim of memory cure, cognitive enhancement, founder intelligence, guaranteed judgment, guaranteed revenue/profit, broad far transfer, causal company impact from recall, or replacement for medical/legal/financial professionals. Method-of-loci and retrieval evidence may justify careful recall-oriented experiments, not business-outcome promises.

## Privacy and security

- Data minimization and connector allowlists by company/domain.
- No secrets/raw PII in cues, prompts, summaries, analytics, screenshots, or palace props.
- Preserve source access controls; a citation is not authorization to reveal content.
- Separate local learning state from authoritative repositories.
- Encrypt stored sensitive metadata under platform policy; log access and revocation.
- Deletion/export controls include revisions and retrieval history where legally permitted.

## Manipulation and dark-pattern review

Reject streak loss, fake XP, shame, hidden objective changes, default consent, urgency timers, disguised ads, forced social sharing, punishment for pause, and engagement optimization that conflicts with founder intent. Progress is optional evidence of learning/application, never a retention weapon.

## Failure behavior

- **Stale:** mark exact last observation; block current-state recommendation.
- **Offline:** preserve last accepted snapshot; allow recall/drafting only.
- **Conflict:** show competing sources and do not choose silently.
- **Low confidence:** narrow the claim or ask for evidence.
- **High consequence:** require named accountable approval in the owning system.
- **Missing receipt:** report “requested” or “drafted,” never “done.”

## Pre-production gates

Trademark/copyright review; threat model; privacy assessment; accessibility audit; falsifiable learning evaluation; dark-pattern review; coach red-team for false authority and goal manipulation; stale/offline tests; consequential-action approval tests; and mobile interruption/resume proof.
