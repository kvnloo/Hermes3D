# Founder Palace product specification

## Purpose

Founder Palace is an evidence-bound founder operating school inside the Hermes3D settlement. It helps a founder orient around an explicit objective, understand connected models, protect attention, apply one model to real company work, inspect evidence, retrieve the model, and revise beliefs.

## Canonical engine

`ORIENT → MAP → FOCUS → ACT → OBSERVE → RETRIEVE → UPDATE`

The seven stages are one engine, not separate branded courses. A session may resume at any stage, but progression records the complete evidence chain.

## Goal hierarchy

Founder intent/identity → objective + guardrails → first principles → connected mental models/tradeoffs → hypotheses/strategies → experiments/actions → leading signals → outcomes → lessons/superseded beliefs.

Sustainable revenue/profit is the default objective when none exists. The founder must see and may replace it. Revenue is evaluated beside customer outcomes, retention, margin, runway, legality, ethics, strategic fit, quality, impact, confounds, and confidence.

## Landmarks

| Landmark | Domain |
|---|---|
| Strategy Observatory | focus, strategy, objective hierarchy, decision quality |
| Customer House | discovery and customer outcome |
| Offer Workshop | positioning, offer and pricing |
| Sales Market | pipeline and willingness-to-pay |
| Finance Vault | cash, runway, margin and scenarios |
| Product Forge | delivery, quality and product evidence |
| Team Hall | hiring, delegation, communication and leadership |
| Experiment Garden | hypotheses, experiments and confounds |
| Review Chamber | weekly review, recall and postmortem |
| Impact Archive | outcomes, impact, lessons and superseded beliefs |

## Coach modes

- **Focus Reset:** ORIENT/MAP/FOCUS; select one protected quest.
- **Objective Sprint:** full loop around revenue/profit or another explicitly selected objective.
- **Decision Room:** MAP/ACT/OBSERVE/UPDATE a consequential decision; never executes it.
- **Skill Lesson:** compact model, scenario retrieval, then direct application.
- **Weekly Review:** OBSERVE/RETRIEVE/UPDATE across commitments and evidence.
- **Recall Walk:** landmark or accessible list retrieval; no company mutation.
- **Postmortem:** outcomes, confounds, lessons, superseded beliefs.

## Session contract

- 5–10 minutes by default; one prompt and one decision at a time.
- Save after every user-confirmed transition; resume from the exact stage.
- Spatial view and list/text view are behaviorally equivalent.
- A focus quest batches shallow notifications and preserves critical/safety alerts.
- The UI labels company fact, hypothesis, recommendation, commitment, and authoritative outcome.
- Each recommendation shows provenance, freshness, confidence, and why it matters.
- Consequential actions always stop at a preview + explicit approval boundary.

## Mastery

Four independent dimensions: (1) knowledge retrieval, (2) scenario discrimination, (3) live application, and (4) outcome reflection. “Mastered” requires evidence in all four; recall alone can never produce mastery. Optional progression is a calm milestone record, not XP.

## Non-goals

No memory improvement product, medical/cognitive claim, autonomous executive, revenue guarantee, generic founder chatbot, copied third-party course, or production activation in this task.

## Product measures

Calibration error, retrieval retention by horizon, application completion, rationale quality, evidence freshness, number of inspected confounds, superseded-belief quality, interruption-safe resume rate, accessibility equivalence, and user-reported agency. Revenue may be observed as a company outcome but is not attributed causally to coaching without an appropriate evaluation design.
