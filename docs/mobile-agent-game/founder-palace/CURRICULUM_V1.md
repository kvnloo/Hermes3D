# Founder skill curriculum v1

## One engine, thirteen skills

Every skill runs through `ORIENT → MAP → FOCUS → ACT → OBSERVE → RETRIEVE → UPDATE`. There are no imported branded mini-courses. Each skill must produce four distinct records: knowledge, scenario practice, live application, and outcome reflection.

| Skill | Landmark | Compact model / tradeoff | Direct company application | Observable evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Focus & strategy | Strategy Observatory | objective, constraint, opportunity cost | choose one protected quest | completion, displaced work, focus quality |
| Customer discovery | Customer House | problem evidence vs stated preference | prepare/review one interview | verbatim evidence, behavior, confounds |
| Positioning | Offer Workshop | audience, alternative, differentiated value | rewrite one positioning hypothesis | comprehension, response, conversion context |
| Offer & pricing | Offer Workshop | value, willingness-to-pay, margin, access | define one ethical price test | paid behavior, retention, margin, objections |
| Sales pipeline | Sales Market | stage criteria and next evidence | inspect one stalled opportunity | stage movement, response, cycle time |
| Finance & runway | Finance Vault | cash, burn, margin, scenario | update one runway assumption | source freshness, variance, downside case |
| Product delivery | Product Forge | scope, quality, cycle time | select one shippable learning slice | acceptance evidence, defects, lead time |
| Experimentation | Experiment Garden | hypothesis, falsifier, confound | design one bounded experiment | predeclared signal, result, confounds |
| Hiring & delegation | Team Hall | ownership, capability, decision rights | delegate one reversible outcome | clarity, handoff, result, rework |
| Communication & leadership | Team Hall | audience, intent, evidence, ask | revise one consequential brief | recipient understanding and action |

## Stage rubric

- **ORIENT:** founder states identity/intent, objective, guardrails, context and confidence.
- **MAP:** founder connects first principles, models, tradeoffs, hypotheses and signals.
- **FOCUS:** one protected objective and completion condition are explicit.
- **ACT:** a real artifact, decision, conversation, calculation, or experiment is touched.
- **OBSERVE:** market/customer/financial plus non-financial evidence and confounds are recorded.
- **RETRIEVE:** founder explains without hints and calibrates confidence before feedback.
- **UPDATE:** model is retained, narrowed, revised, or superseded with rationale.

## Review rhythm

Schedule retrieval based on desired retention horizon, observed success, confidence calibration and whether direct application has occurred. A successful recall may lengthen the knowledge interval, but missing application/outcome evidence keeps mastery incomplete. A surprising real outcome may trigger immediate UPDATE even when recall was perfect.

## Example: Strategy Observatory

Objective: improve sustainable profit without violating customer-outcome and quality guardrails. Model: “A strategy is a constrained choice, not a list.” Focus: choose one 10-minute tradeoff. Act: identify one project to stop or defer. Observe: recovered capacity, delivery effect, customer evidence and adverse impact. Retrieve: explain the tradeoff tomorrow without the card. Update: revise the strategy map when evidence contradicts the assumption.
