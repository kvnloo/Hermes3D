# User journeys

All journeys use one engine: ORIENT → MAP → FOCUS → ACT → OBSERVE → RETRIEVE → UPDATE. Mobile presents one prompt/decision at a time; desktop may show the map and evidence rail simultaneously. Spatial and list/text controls are equivalent.

## Onboarding

1. Explain coach limits, provenance classes, privacy, and that outcomes are not guaranteed.
2. Founder enters intent/identity and reviews an editable default objective of sustainable revenue/profit.
3. Founder adds guardrails: runway, customer outcome, legality/ethics, quality, impact, time, and personal constraints.
4. Connect data sources with least privilege; show available, missing, and stale context.
5. Choose spatial or list/text mode, motion preference, prompt density and interruption policy.
6. Walk Strategy Observatory once; encode one original model and retrieve it.
7. Apply it to one reversible live decision; no consequential action is executed.

## Focus Reset

1. ORIENT: show current objective, guardrails, stale sources and open commitments.
2. MAP: identify the highest-leverage uncertainty or bottleneck.
3. FOCUS: founder chooses one 5–10 minute completion condition.
4. Batch shallow notifications; retain critical alerts and visible exit.
5. ACT: touch the real artifact/decision.
6. OBSERVE: record immediate evidence or define the next observable signal.
7. Save interruption-safe checkpoint; resume at the exact prompt.

## Objective Sprint (Revenue or another objective)

1. Founder confirms the objective; revenue/profit is never silently selected.
2. Map willingness-to-pay, retention, margin, customer outcome, runway, ethics, quality, strategic fit and impact.
3. Select one hypothesis and one bounded experiment.
4. Preview action; request explicit approval if consequential.
5. Observe signals and confounds; distinguish correlation from causal claim.
6. Retrieve the model and update the hypothesis, including superseded belief.

## Weekly Review

1. Verify data freshness and receipts.
2. Review objective/guardrail movement, commitments and focus quality.
3. Inspect market, customer, financial and non-financial evidence.
4. Retrieve one model before seeing notes; record confidence.
5. Compare prediction with outcome and confounds.
6. Retain, narrow, revise or supersede the belief.
7. Choose one next focus quest and an if/then implementation intention.

## Recall Walk

1. Choose spatial landmark or equivalent ordered list.
2. Show cue only; founder explains the model and tradeoff without hints.
3. Record confidence before feedback.
4. Reveal accepted statement, provenance, freshness and linked application.
5. Ask what new evidence would change it.
6. Schedule follow-up by desired horizon and observed difficulty.
7. Never interpret recall success as business correctness or mastery.

## Decision Room

1. State decision, deadline, owner, reversibility, objective and guardrails.
2. Map options, assumptions, tradeoffs, base-rate/context evidence and missing data.
3. Coach offers labeled recommendations and non-action alternative.
4. Founder chooses or defers; execution remains outside the coach unless explicitly approved in the owning system.
5. Schedule observable signals and review date.
6. Post-outcome, separate decision quality from luck and update the model.

## Stale/offline context

1. Banner shows last accepted observation and unavailable sources.
2. Current-state assertions and dependent consequential recommendations are disabled.
3. Founder may continue a clearly historical Recall Walk, draft hypotheses, or queue evidence questions.
4. Queued work stores no secrets/raw PII and does not claim completion.
5. On reconnection, revalidate sources before showing or applying recommendations.

## Accessibility and interruption acceptance

- Every landmark, cue, relation, status and action has semantic text.
- Logical focus order, keyboard activation, screen-reader announcements, contrast, zoom, reduced motion and non-spatial route are required.
- No time-limited recall by default.
- Pause/Back/reload restores stage, prompt, draft response and safe scroll position without duplicating commitments.
